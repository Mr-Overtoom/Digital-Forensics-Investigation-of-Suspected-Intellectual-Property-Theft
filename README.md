# Digital-Forensics-Investigation-of-Suspected-Intellectual-Property-Theft
This project demonstrates a digital forensic investigation of a forensic disk image belonging to a former employee suspected of unauthorized access to confidential corporate intellectual property.  

Using the Autopsy Digital Forensics Platform, the investigation focused on identifying evidence of intellectual property (IP) theft, recovering deleted artifacts, examining file system evidence, and documenting findings relevant to an insider threat investigation.

Investigation Objectives
Conduct a forensic examination using accepted digital forensics methodologies
Recover deleted files and artifacts
Identify evidence of unauthorized possession of confidential information
Document evidentiary findings
Determine whether digital evidence supports allegations of intellectual property theft

Scenario
A former employee of an engineering firm was suspected of accessing and retaining confidential intellectual property outside the scope of authorized job responsibilities.
The investigation sought to answer the following question: Is there digital evidence demonstrating possession of protected corporate intellectual property?
